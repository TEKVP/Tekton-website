About Tekton

A premier manufacturer of state-of-the-art vertical transportation.

Backed by over two decades of engineering mastery, we deliver custom-designed elevators and escalators engineered for absolute safety, energy efficiency and smooth aesthetic integration into modern architecture.

  • 20+ years of engineering mastery
  • 300+ successful installations commissioned
  • 100% compliance with IS 14665 electric traction safety standards

Our mission

To deliver next-generation vertical transportation systems powered by smart IoT telemetry and AI predictive maintenance, prioritising safety, precision, and zero-downtime reliability.

Our vision

To become the global leader in AI-driven vertical mobility — delivering world-class technology, uncompromised safety, and market-leading value.
